What is Project Viridis?

Project Viridis is a student-driven initiative exploring how smart technology can transform the way our cities handle waste. From sensor-equipped bins to AI-powered recycling systems, we believe that cleaner cities start with smarter choices — and that every one of us plays a part. What is urban eco-gardens?

Smart waste systems use sensors, data, and technology to monitor bin fill levels, optimise collection routes, and cut down on unnecessary trips — making cities cleaner and more efficient.

How does recycling help the city?

Proper recycling reduces landfill overflow, lowers carbon emissions from waste trucks, and turns old materials into new resources — saving money and protecting the environment at the same time.

Can I make a difference as an individual?

Absolutely! Sorting your waste correctly, reducing single-use plastics, and staying informed are all powerful steps. When communities act together, the impact on a city's sustainability is massive.

What makes a city "smart"?

A smart city uses connected technology — like IoT sensors, real-time data, and automation — to manage resources more efficiently. Waste management is one of the most visible and impactful areas where this is happening today.
