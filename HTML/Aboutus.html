Who We Are

About Us

We at project viridis are a group of students who are passionate about making a positive impact on the environment, particularly in the area of waste management. We believe that by educating and empowering individuals to take action towards sustainability, we can create a cleaner and healthier planet for future generations. Our website is a platform for sharing information, resources, and tools to help people reduce their waste and live more eco-friendly lives.

Our Purpose

Aim & Mission

Our aim is to educate and empower individuals to take action towards creating a more sustainable future. Through our website, we provide resources, tips, and tools to help people reduce their waste and live more eco-friendly lives. We believe that small changes can make a big difference, and we are committed to inspiring and supporting our community in their journey towards sustainability.
