How To Garden

Your step-by-step guide to growing a thriving eco-garden from scratch.

Getting Started

Anyone Can Grow Something

Whether you have a balcony, a backyard, or just a windowsill — gardening is one of the most rewarding things you can do for yourself and the planet. Follow our guide below and you'll have your first eco-garden up and running in no time.

The Process

Step-by-Step Planting Guide

Follow these steps and you'll be harvesting your own greens in weeks.

1
Choose Your Space

Find a spot that gets at least 6 hours of sunlight per day. This could be a garden bed, raised planter, pot, or even a sunny windowsill. South-facing spots tend to get the most light in Singapore's climate.

2
Pick the Right Soil

Good soil is the foundation of a healthy garden. Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. Avoid regular ground soil for containers — it compacts too easily and can suffocate roots.

3
Select Your Plants

For beginners, start with fast-growing, forgiving plants like lettuce, basil, chilli, or kangkong. These thrive in tropical climates and give you results quickly — which keeps you motivated to keep going.

4
Plant Your Seeds or Seedlings

Sow seeds at the depth shown on the packet — usually 2–3× the seed's diameter. Space them out to allow airflow. If using seedlings, gently loosen the roots before placing them in the hole and firm the soil around the base.

5
Water Consistently

Water at the base of the plant, not the leaves. Most plants prefer consistently moist (not waterlogged) soil. In Singapore's heat, morning watering is best — it lets plants absorb moisture before the midday sun kicks in.

6
Feed & Maintain

After 2–3 weeks, start feeding with a diluted liquid fertiliser or compost tea every 1–2 weeks. Remove dead leaves, watch for pests, and give climbing plants like beans a simple trellis to grow on.

7
Harvest & Repeat

Harvest leafy greens by cutting outer leaves and letting the plant continue growing. For fruit-bearing plants like chillies, pick regularly to encourage more growth. Save seeds from your best plants to start the next cycle!

Plant Guide

What Should You Grow?

Overview of Plants for Gardens.

🌿
Beginner
Herbs

Basil, mint, pandan, and lemongrass are almost impossible to kill and grow back after cutting. Perfect for a windowsill.

🥬
Beginner
Leafy Greens

Kangkong, spinach, and kai lan grow fast and can be harvested repeatedly. Great for small pots or raised beds.

🌶️
Intermediate
Chillies & Peppers

Chilli plants thrive in Singapore's heat. They take a few weeks but reward you with harvests for months.

🍅
Intermediate
Cherry Tomatoes

Cherry tomatoes love the sun and produce heavily. Use a cage or trellis to support the vines as they grow.

🥒
Intermediate
Cucumbers

Fast-growing climbers that need a trellis. Keep them well-watered and you'll have cucumbers within 6–8 weeks of planting.

🎋
Advanced
Fruit Trees

Dwarf banana, papaya, and starfruit can be grown in large containers. They take patience but transform any garden.

Equipment

Tools You'll Need

You don't need much — here's what actually matters.

🪣
Watering Can or Hose

Essential. Use a can with a rose head for gentle watering of seedlings.

🧤
Gardening Gloves

Protect your hands from soil, thorns, and insects while working.

🪚
Trowel & Hand Fork

For digging, planting, and loosening soil in containers or small beds.

✂️
Pruning Shears

Clean cuts encourage healthy regrowth and stop disease from spreading.

🪴
Pots & Planters

Make sure they have drainage holes. Size matters — bigger pots = more root space = bigger plants.

🌱
Potting Mix & Compost

Good quality soil is non-negotiable. Mix in compost for extra nutrients.

Pro Tips

Tips for Success

🌤️ Start Small

One or two pots beats an overwhelming setup. Master the basics before expanding your garden.

💧 Water in the Morning

Morning watering reduces evaporation and keeps leaves dry overnight, preventing fungal issues.

♻️ Compost Your Scraps

Kitchen scraps like veggie peels and eggshells can be composted and returned to your garden as free fertiliser.

🐛 Check for Pests Weekly

Catching pests early is far easier than dealing with an infestation. Flip leaves and check stems regularly.

📓 Keep a Garden Journal

Note what you planted, when, and how it grew. This is the fastest way to learn what works in your specific space.

Watch Out

Common Mistakes

🚫 Overwatering

The #1 killer of beginner gardens. If the top inch of soil is still moist, don't water yet. Roots need air too.

🚫 Too Little Sunlight

Most vegetables and herbs need 6+ hours of direct sun. A shady balcony will frustrate you — choose shade-tolerant plants instead.

🚫 Planting Too Close Together

Crowded plants compete for nutrients and block airflow, leading to disease. Always check the recommended spacing.

🚫 Skipping Fertiliser

Potting mix runs out of nutrients fast. Without regular feeding, growth stalls and plants become pale and weak.

🚫 Giving Up Too Early

Germination can take 1–2 weeks. Slow growth is normal at the start — patience is the most important gardening skill.
